# User Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective date:** September 16, 2026

If you have interacted with Trial App through Instagram and want data associated with your interaction deleted, follow the steps below.

## How to Request Deletion
1. Email faizan.shamsi@arwentech.com.
2. Use the subject line: **Instagram Data Deletion Request**.
3. Include your Instagram username and a brief description of the interaction you want deleted.
4. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials.

## What We Will Do
After verifying the request as reasonably necessary, we will delete or anonymize data under our control associated with the relevant Instagram interaction, unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance reasons.

## Processing Time
We aim to complete valid deletion requests within 30 days.

## Third-Party Data
Some information may also be held independently by third-party platforms such as Meta/Instagram or other service providers. Their own privacy and deletion processes may apply separately.

## Contact
For deletion requests or questions, contact: faizan.shamsi@arwentech.com
