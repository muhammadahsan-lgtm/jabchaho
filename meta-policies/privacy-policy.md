# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 16, 2026

Trial App uses the Instagram API to receive and respond to messages sent to our connected Instagram business account.

## Information We Process
When you send a message to our Instagram account, we may process information made available through the Instagram API, including your Instagram-scoped user identifier, message content, message timestamps, and related messaging metadata.

## How We Use Information
We use this information only to provide automated customer-support responses, answer questions about our business, services, pricing, availability, policies, and other information contained in our business knowledge base, and to operate, test, secure, and improve the messaging integration.

## AI Processing
Message content may be processed by an AI service used by the application to generate responses based on our business information. We do not intentionally use customer messages to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

## Data Sharing
We may share data only with service providers required to operate the messaging workflow, such as Meta/Instagram, our automation platform, and our AI service provider. We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for testing, support, security, troubleshooting, and operation of the service, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Your Choices
You may stop interacting with the automated messaging service at any time. You may also request deletion of data associated with your interaction by following our Data Deletion Instructions.

## Security
We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The updated effective date will be shown at the top of this page.
